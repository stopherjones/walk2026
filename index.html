Route notices

Last checked 23 Sep 2026. Diversions can change, so re-check the official page the day before.

Temporary diversion: stair upgrade south of Robin Hood’s Bay Listed on the official Cleveland Way page as a temporary diversion for stair upgrade works. Check the official page for the current detail and end date before you set off.
Robin Hood’s Bay erosion diversion (in place since July 2021) The Cleveland Way and England Coast Path are diverted inland at the eastern end of Mount Pleasant North, across Rocket Post Field and down an accessible ramp onto a short stretch of the Cinder Track into the village.

The other diversions listed on the official page (Skelton and the Lady Chapel) are outside the Scarborough to Whitby section.

08:30

1. Scarborough (Cinder Track Start)

Distance: 0.0 miles (Start point)

Begin walk along the historic disused railway line (The Cinder Track), offering a smooth, gradual trail out of Scarborough towards Scalby.

10:45

2. Hayburn Wyke / Cloughton

Distance: 6.5 miles

A scenic wooded section. Quick coffee break - probably bring your own in case the pub isn't open.

Suggested Food & Drink

Hayburn Wyke Inn
Hours: Open daily 11:00 - 21:30
12:15 - 12:45

3. Ravenscar

Distance: 10.5 miles

Midpoint milestone high on the cliff tops. Transition point from the Cinder Track onto the rugged Cleveland Way coastal cliff path.

Suggested Food & Drink

Ravenscar Tearooms (Maps)
Hours: Open 10:00 - 16:00
14:00 - 15:00

4. Robin Hood's Bay

Distance: 14.0 miles

Steep scenic descent into the historic cobblestone fishing village and beach harbour. Designated stop for main sit-down lunch.

Suggested Food & Drink

The Bay Hotel (Maps) or The Smugglers Inn (Maps)
Hours: Open 11:00 - 23:30
17:30 - 18:00

5. Whitby Abbey

Distance: 21.0 miles

Climactic arrival at the dramatic Abbey headland. Descend the famous 199 steps to the old town.

18:15+

6. Whitby Harbour

Distance: 21.2 miles (Finish)

Finish the route with fish & chips overlooking Whitby Harbour, near to Church Street car park.

Suggested Food & Drink

Abbey Wharf (Maps)
Hours: Open daily 11:00 - 22:00+

1. Scarborough (Cinder Track Start)

Distance: 0.0 miles (Start point)

Begin walk along the historic disused railway line (The Cinder Track), offering a smooth, gradual trail out of Scarborough towards Scalby.

10:45

2. Hayburn Wyke / Cloughton

Distance: 6.5 miles

A scenic wooded section leading towards the coast. Short rest and coffee break location before tackling the next stretch to Ravenscar.

Suggested Food & Drink

Hayburn Wyke Inn
Hours: Open daily 11:00 - 21:30
Recommendation: Traditional 18th-century pub with beer garden. Ideal for a morning hot drink, cold soft drink, or light snack.
12:15 - 12:45

3. Ravenscar

Distance: 10.5 miles

Midpoint milestone high on the cliff tops. Transition point from the Cinder Track onto the rugged Cleveland Way coastal cliff path.

Suggested Food & Drink

Ravenscar Tearooms (Maps)
Hours: Open 10:00 - 16:00
Recommendation: Freshly baked cakes, fruit scones with jam & cream, hot soup, tea, and coffee.
14:00 - 15:00

4. Robin Hood's Bay

Distance: 14.0 miles

Steep scenic descent into the historic cobblestone fishing village and beach harbour. Designated stop for main sit-down lunch.

Suggested Food & Drink

The Bay Hotel (Maps) or The Smugglers Inn (Maps)
Hours: Open 11:00 - 23:30
Recommendation: Hearty pub lunch, fresh seafood, fish & chips, and local Yorkshire cask ales overlooking the bay.
17:30 - 18:00

5. Whitby Abbey

Distance: 21.0 miles

Climactic arrival at the dramatic headland overlooking Whitby. Take in the cliff views before descending the famous 199 steps to the old town.

18:15+

6. Whitby Harbour & Old Town

Distance: 21.2 miles (Finish)

Route finish line along the harbour. Time to celebrate, unwind, and enjoy dinner while designated drivers return to Scarborough to retrieve Car 2.

Suggested Food & Drink

Abbey Wharf (Maps)
Hours: Open daily 11:00 - 22:00+
Recommendation: Prime Whitby fish & chips, seafood platters, steaks, and drinks with panoramic harbour views.
